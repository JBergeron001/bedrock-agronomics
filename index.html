WHAT WE TEST

Two focus areas, tested where they matter.

Based in St. Louis, we serve growers and input companies across the U.S., including the West, and design trials that public research programs can fund.

Mineral amendments Pozzolan and other natural minerals, soil conditioners and reclamation blends: how much they return, at what rate, and on which soils.
Grasses and forage on degraded soils Turf, forage and specialty crops on degraded, reclaimed and claypan ground, where better soil pays back fastest.
Grant-funded field programs Trials designed with industry and university partners to meet public research program standards, with reviewer-ready reporting.
